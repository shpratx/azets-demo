UploadFile upload fails validation — what formats are accepted? ▲
ALM accepts .xlsx, .xls, .csv, and .ods formats. Common validation failures include: (1) Missing required column headers — your file must contain at least Account Code, Account Name, and Balance columns. (2) File size exceeds 50MB limit — split large files by division. (3) Encoding issues — ensure files are saved as UTF-8. Download the template file for the correct format.
MappingAI mapping shows very low confidence scores (<60%) across most accounts ▲
Low confidence across the board usually indicates a structural mismatch between your chart of accounts and the Azets reference framework. Check: (1) Your account codes use a non-standard prefix format — go to Settings → Mapping Profile to configure prefix normalisation. (2) Account descriptions are too abbreviated — the AI uses semantic analysis and needs descriptive names. (3) You may be using a legacy CoA version — contact your ALM administrator to update the reference set.
