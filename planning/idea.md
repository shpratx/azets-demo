**Idea Title:** Automated Ledger Mapping Tool for Acquired Firms on Azets Cozone

**Concept Overview:** This software solution automates the mapping of legacy bookkeeping accounts from acquired firms to Azets' standardized master ledger system within the Cozone platform. By eliminating manual Excel-based translation, the tool ensures seamless integration, reduces errors, and accelerates financial onboarding during mergers and acquisitions. It provides a user-friendly interface for finance teams to upload legacy account structures, intelligently matches them to Azets' ledger codes, and generates audit-ready mapping reports.

**Problem:** Manual translation of legacy bookkeeping accounts into Azets' master ledger system is time-consuming, error-prone, and often relies on Excel spreadsheets, leading to costly mistakes and delays during post-acquisition integration.

**Target Audience:** Finance and accounting teams at Azets and other mid-to-large accounting firms managing mergers and acquisitions, especially those responsible for integrating acquired firms' financial systems.

**Core Solution:** The tool leverages AI and rule-based algorithms to automatically map legacy account codes to Azets' master ledger, providing suggestions, flagging ambiguities, and allowing for manual overrides. It generates detailed mapping reports and integrates directly with Cozone for seamless ledger updates.
 
**Unique Value Proposition:** Unlike generic data migration tools, this solution is tailored for Azets' ledger structure and Cozone platform, offering intelligent mapping, auditability, and direct integration—dramatically reducing manual effort and translation errors.

**Key Success Metric:** Reduction in account mapping errors and time spent per acquisition integration project.

**Monetization Model:** Subscription-based SaaS model for Azets and partner firms, with tiered pricing based on the number of integrations and volume of accounts processed.
